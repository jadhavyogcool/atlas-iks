01 About the Conference

Bridging civilizational heritage with contemporary science, design and governance.

India's intellectual heritage is a magnificent confluence of rigorous scientific inquiry, mathematical breakthroughs, holistic healthcare, sustainable architecture, and profound philosophical insights. As our nation charts its journey toward Viksit Bharat @ 2047, the National Education Policy (NEP 2020) places Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) at the very core of multidisciplinary innovation.

Hosted by ATLAS SkillTech University at BKC, Mumbai, From Roots to Relevance: Indian Knowledge Systems for a New India provides a premier national platform bringing together researchers, scientists, designers, technologists, and educators to discover, scientifically validate, and transform traditional knowledge into scalable solutions for modern global challenges.

About ATLAS SkillTech University

ATLAS SkillTech University is a state-of-the-art private multidisciplinary university established under Maharashtra Act No. XV, located in the global commercial hub of Equinox Business Park, Off Bandra-Kurla Complex (BKC), Kurla West, Mumbai.

Built on the pillars of Design (ISDI), Management & Entrepreneurship (ISME), Emerging Technology (uGDX), and Law, ATLAS bridges industry and academia, equipping the next generation with future-ready skills, critical inquiry, and cultural grounding.

02 Conference Theme

Honouring the Past. Reimagining the Present. Envisioning Tomorrow.

The tripartite theme encapsulates the living continuum of Indian Knowledge Systems. It is not merely a retrospective study of historical milestones, but an active, dynamic paradigm that draws strength from ancestral roots, subjects indigenous concepts to contemporary scientific rigor, and projects them into the sustainable technologies of tomorrow.

Pillars of Contemporary Relevance
Epistemological Rigor Scientific Validation Sustainable Technologies Computational Linguistics Viksit Bharat @ 2047
A

Honouring the Past

Preserving, translating, and understanding ancient Sanskrit treatises in astronomy, mathematics, medicine, architecture and metallurgy.

B

Reimagining the Present

Benchmarking indigenous wisdom against modern empirical standards, design methodologies, and public policy under NEP 2020.

C

Envisioning Tomorrow

Pioneering AI algorithms, green architecture, circular economy, and regenerative wellness for a resilient, self-reliant New India.

06 Indicative Tracks

8 Interdisciplinary Tracks. Over 40 Research Directions.

Tracks are indicative and subject to final review and specialization by the Conference Advisory Committee.

TRACK 01

Ganita & Khagola — Mathematics, Astronomy & Physical Sciences

  • Aryabhata, Brahmagupta, Madhava: Calculus & Infinite Series in Indian Mathematics
  • Astronomy, Calendrical Systems (Panchanga) & Celestial Mechanics
  • Traditional Metallurgy (Rasashastra), Wootz Steel & Ancient Chemistry
  • Ship Building, Navigation (Navgati) & Marine Engineering in Ancient India
  • Epistemology and Proofs (Upapatti) in Classical Indian Sciences
TRACK 02

Ayurveda, Yoga & Holistic Healthcare Systems

  • Integrative Medicine: Combining Allopathy with Ayurveda & Siddha
  • Pharmacognosy, Ethnobotany & Conservation of Medicinal Flora
  • Mind-Body Sciences: Yoga Psychology, Neurobiology & Meditation
  • Preventive Nutrition (Ahara-Vidhi) & Chronobiology (Dinacharya / Ritucharya)
  • Clinical Validation Protocols & Standardized Herbal Formulations
TRACK 03

Sthapatya & Vastu — Sustainable Architecture & Ecology

  • Sacred Geometry, Vastu Shastra & Climate-Responsive Built Forms
  • Traditional Water Harvesting Systems (Kunds, Stepwells, Baoris, Ahar-Pyne)
  • Vernacular Architecture, Local Materials & Low-Carbon Footprint Construction
  • Urban Settlement Patterns in Indus-Saraswati & Ancient Temple Towns
  • Ecological Ethics, Sacred Groves & Biodiversity Conservation
TRACK 04

Arthashastra, Rajaniti & Indigenous Governance Models

  • Kautilya’s Arthashastra: Statecraft, Public Administration & Diplomacy
  • Dharmic Leadership, Stakeholder Capitalism & Ethical Governance
  • Rural Economy, Village Autonomy & Artisanal Guilds (Shrenis)
  • Sustainable Trade Routes, Maritime Commerce & Global Networks
  • Conflict Resolution, Social Harmony & Jurisprudence (Vyavahara)
TRACK 05

Linguistics, Computational Sanskrit & Artificial Intelligence

  • Panini’s Ashtadhyayi: Algorithmic Grammar & Formal Language Theory
  • Sanskrit as an Ideal Foundation for Natural Language Processing (NLP)
  • Knowledge Representation, Nyaya Logic & Ontological Modelling in AI
  • Digital Preservation & Optical Character Recognition (OCR) for Manuscripts
  • LLMs and Multi-Modal AI for Vernacular Indian Languages & IKS Repositories
TRACK 06

Kala, Shilpa & Design Aesthetics (Natyashastra to Modern Design)

  • Natyashastra, Rasa Theory & Indian Aesthetics in Contemporary Design
  • Traditional Craftsmanship, Handloom Textiles & Natural Dyeing Techniques
  • Semiotics of Indian Temple Art, Sculpture & Sacred Iconography
  • Folk Arts, Living Traditions & Intangible Cultural Heritage Preservation
  • Modern Industrial & Graphic Design Inspired by Traditional Artifacts
TRACK 07

Pedagogy, NEP 2020 & Transdisciplinary Curriculum

  • Implementing Indian Knowledge Systems in Higher Education Curricula
  • Experiential Learning Frameworks Inspired by Takshashila & Nalanda
  • Transdisciplinary Research Methodologies Bridging IKS and STEM
  • Teacher Training & Faculty Development in Indigenous Epistemology
  • Open Educational Resources (OER) & Digital Archives for IKS
TRACK 08

Viksit Bharat @ 2047: IKS for National Growth & Global Leadership

  • Aligning Indigenous Innovation with Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
  • Atmanirbhar Bharat: Traditional Technologies for Self-Reliance
  • Indian Contributions to Global Peace, Environmental Stewardship & Ethics
  • Traditional Knowledge Digital Library (TKDL) & Intellectual Property Rights
  • Youth Engagement, Startups & Entrepreneurship Rooted in Heritage

Author Guidelines & Submission Kit

1. Manuscript Format & Length

  • Full research papers must be between 6 to 8 pages in standard double-column format (IEEE / Springer conference format).
  • Submissions are accepted in English or Sanskrit (with English abstract and standard Roman IAST transliteration).
  • Templates in LaTeX and Microsoft Word will be provided upon official portal opening.

2. Originality & Plagiarism Threshold

  • All submissions must report original, previously unpublished research that is not currently under review elsewhere.
  • Strict similarity threshold: Plagiarism must be below 10% (excluding references) via Turnitin / Urkund.
  • Rigorous scholarly citation of ancient manuscripts, commentaries, and modern scientific literature is required.

3. Double-Blind Peer Review

  • All manuscripts undergo blind peer evaluation by two independent domain experts on technical depth, methodology, and relevance.
  • The initial submitted PDF must not disclose author names, institutional affiliations, or email addresses.
  • Author identity is submitted exclusively via the submission portal metadata.

4. ISBN Proceedings & Journal Fast-Tracking

  • All registered, presented papers will be published in the official conference proceedings volume with a registered ISBN.
  • Outstanding papers from each track will be invited for expansion and publication in Scopus / UGC-CARE indexed partner journals.
